# ERP BY ANMOL V2 Modules

## Platform & Core
- Multi-tenancy and school onboarding
- Identity, authentication, refresh sessions and RBAC
- School settings and module controls
- Audit logs and analytics
- Notifications and background jobs

## School Operations
- Students, guardians, teachers and staff
- Admissions and custom forms
- Academic sessions, classes, sections, subjects and teacher mapping
- Conflict-aware timetable
- Student and faculty attendance
- Assignments and submissions
- Examinations, marks, publishing and OASES evaluation
- Report cards and templates
- Fees, invoices, payments, receipts and refunds
- Library issue/return/fines
- HR payroll and salary processing
- Student/staff leave workflows
- Notices, grievances, chat and knowledge resources
- Dynamic documents and public verification
- CSV/XLSX-style import pipeline endpoints
- Biometric device/mapping/punch ingestion

Every school-owned module is designed to execute through centralized tenant context and server-side permission checks.
